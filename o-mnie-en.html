[ 003 — the story ]

First,
I feel.

Music is a conversation between a person, an instrument and a room.

I am not looking for perfectly polished sounds. I am looking for sounds with weight, texture and direction. That is why piano, synthesizer, silence, chance and another person’s reaction matter equally to me.

I play, compose and design sound until technique disappears and emotion remains.

[ 004 — in the room ]
“Sound has a temperature.”

The most interesting things happen just before a decision. I leave room for that moment.

[ 005 — how I work ]
01

I listen to context

Demo, lyrics, stage, people. Before I play, I want to know what should stay in the memory.

02

I build a palette

I choose instruments and textures for the emotion, not the other way around. Less preset, more character.

03

I react to the moment

Nothing is closed on stage. A good setup leaves room for an answer.
